MNIST Classification with Quantum Neural Networks

PyTorch · Classiq SDK · Quantum Machine Learning

A hybrid quantum neural network for handwritten-digit classification, combining a classical training pipeline with quantum circuits built in the Classiq SDK. The project covers data encoding, circuit design, training, and evaluation, and examines where quantum layers help and where they do not.

Vehicle Detection in Dense Urban Traffic

Computer Vision · YOLO · Object Detection

An evaluation of YOLO architectures for vehicle detection in Bangladeshi urban traffic, where cluttered scenes, unconventional vehicle types, and heavy occlusion break assumptions built into models trained on Western road data.
