Frequently asked questions

Can international students apply?

Most listed universities accept international PhD applicants, but funding and eligibility vary by lab. Confirm with the professor and the official program page before applying.

Which start terms does this list cover?

Spring and Fall 2027. Fall 2027 is the most realistic intake for international applicants because many Spring deadlines have passed.

Are these positions funded?

Several professors describe fully funded positions, but stipend, tuition coverage, and duration are not always stated. Ask for the written funding package.
