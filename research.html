Research

Quantum Computing, Trustworthy AI, and Autonomous Systems

I am a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science at Tulane University and a researcher in Dr. Jiyuan Wang's lab. My work focuses on reliable, measurable, and practical computing systems.

Research Areas

Quantum Computing

Quantum algorithms, quantum machine learning, quantum kernels, and practical approaches for using quantum resources in computing.

Quantum Software Engineering

Software-development methods, testing, verification, benchmarking, and engineering tools for dependable quantum applications.

Autonomous Systems

Intelligent perception, decision-making, computer vision, and dependable software for autonomous and real-time systems.

Trustworthy Language Models

LLM safety, hallucination detection and mitigation, reliability, and responsible behavior in large language models.

Language Model Evaluation

Evaluation design, factual reliability, robustness, and methods for measuring reasoning ability and limitations in language models.

Applied Machine Learning

Machine learning for computer vision, time-series modeling, classification, forecasting, and scientific data analysis.
