About

I began my PhD in Computer Science at Tulane University in August 2026. I work on quantum software engineering, quantum algorithms, and quantum testing, advised by Dr. Jiyuan Wang. My other interests run to automated system design for dense, unstructured traffic, weather prediction and analysis, and running small language models on edge devices. Before coming to Tulane I was a Lecturer in Computer Science and Engineering at Premier University, Chittagong.

Research Interests

  • Quantum software engineering. I study the development practices, tooling, and benchmarking that quantum programs need before anyone can depend on them.
  • Quantum algorithms. I am interested in designing and analyzing algorithms that make genuine use of quantum resources rather than merely invoking them.
  • Quantum testing. I work on how to verify correctness in programs whose output is probabilistic and expensive to observe.
  • Automated system design. I am interested in perception and control for dense, unstructured traffic, where the assumptions behind systems built elsewhere tend to break down.
  • Weather prediction and analysis. I work on forecasting and modeling environmental and climate indicators from observational data.
  • Small language models on edge devices. I am interested in what it takes to run capable models under tight compute and memory budgets.

Projects

Autonomous Navigation in Bangladesh: A Multi-Camera Perception-to-Action Approach

This project is funded by a grant from the Institute of Research and Publications at Premier University, Chattogram, awarded in 2026.

I am building a multi-camera perception-to-action pipeline for road conditions in which dense traffic and unstructured lane behavior break the assumptions built into systems developed elsewhere.

MNIST Classification with Quantum Neural Networks

I built this project using PyTorch and the Classiq SDK.

It is a hybrid quantum neural network for digit classification, and it covers the full path from data encoding through circuit design, training, and evaluation.
