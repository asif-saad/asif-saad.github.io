I am a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science at Tulane University, working in Dr. Jiyuan Wang's lab. Research profile

My work focuses on reliable and practical computing systems—from quantum software and quantum-enabled learning to autonomous systems and the evaluation of modern language models. I am particularly interested in developing methods that make advanced AI systems more dependable, measurable, and useful in real-world settings.

Quantum Computing

Quantum algorithms, quantum machine learning, and the practical development of quantum-enabled computing systems.

Quantum Software Engineering

Methods, tools, testing practices, and software-engineering principles for building reliable quantum applications.

Autonomous Systems

Intelligent perception, decision-making, and dependable software for autonomous and real-time systems.

Trustworthy & Safe LLMs

LLM safety, hallucination detection and mitigation, reliability, and responsible model behavior.

Benchmarking & LLM Reasoning

Evaluation design, factual reliability, robustness, and the reasoning capabilities and limitations of language models.

Applied Machine Learning

Computer vision, time-series modeling, and machine-learning methods for scientific and real-world data.
