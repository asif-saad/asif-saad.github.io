About

I began my PhD in Computer Science at Tulane University in August 2026. I work on quantum software engineering, quantum algorithms, and quantum testing, advised by Dr. Jiyuan Wang. My other interests run to automated system design for dense, unstructured traffic, weather prediction and analysis, and running small language models on edge devices. Before Tulane I was a Lecturer in Computer Science and Engineering at Premier University, Chittagong.

Research Interests

  • Quantum software engineering — development practices, tooling, and benchmarking for dependable quantum programs
  • Quantum algorithms — design and analysis of algorithms that make real use of quantum resources
  • Quantum testing — verifying correctness in programs whose output is probabilistic and expensive to observe
  • Automated system design — perception and control for dense, unstructured traffic
  • Weather prediction and analysis — forecasting and modeling of environmental and climate indicators
  • Small language models on edge devices — running capable models under tight compute and memory budgets
